Core Idea

Standard neural world models are sample-inefficient and opaque, while propositional symbolic methods suffer combinatorial explosion as environments grow. We build symbolic causal world models entirely online by integrating continual model learning and repair directly inside the agent's decision loop, using Meta-Interpretive Learning (MIL) with dynamic predicate invention.

Key Takeaways

01

Dynamic Predicate Invention

Automatically invents semantically meaningful, reusable abstractions from raw observations, assembling a transparent hierarchy of disentangled concepts.

02

Lifted Relational Scaling

Operates over lifted first-order relations rather than flat propositional states, scaling cleanly to complex relational dynamics without combinatorial blow-up.

03

Orders-of-Magnitude Efficiency

Achieves sample efficiency orders of magnitude higher than the established neural PPO baseline while preserving full interpretability and online repairability.
